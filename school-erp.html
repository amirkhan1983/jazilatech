School ERP

Jazila School ERP is a complete school management solution designed to simplify everyday school operations from one centralized platform. Manage students, teachers, attendance, academics, examinations, transport, and hostel activities with ease.

With a clean, user-friendly interface, the ERP helps reduce manual work, keep records organized, and make important information easily accessible to school administrators and staff.

Modernize your school management with Jazila School ERP and bring your essential academic and administrative operations together in one efficient digital platform.

  • Student Admission & Records
  • Fees Management & Online Payment
  • Attendance & Timetable
  • Exams, Report Card & Results
  • Staff, Payroll & Communication
